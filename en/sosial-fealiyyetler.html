Social Activities

Founder Club's social projects, charity events and community support activities.

Charity Fair

On June 8, 2024, the Founder Club together with the Healthy Generation Public Association organized a charity fair.

A New Year Event Was Held for Children with Down Syndrome

On January 3, an event was held at the Başkent restaurant, organized by the Public Union of Nur D/S Children and supported by the Founder Club.

A charity event for children with Down syndrome was organized by the joint organization of "Founder Club" and "Founder Woman Club".

The charity event was held with the support of Founder Club, Founder Women Club Board of Directors, VECON Consulting LLC and Başkent Restaurant.

The handiwork of children in need of our care will be exhibited and sold

On September 26, children's handicrafts were presented in partnership with Founder Women Club, Accelerated Career Academy and Başkent Restaurant.

A charity event organized for children with Down syndrome

It was organized with the partners of Founder Club and Founder Women Club to support children in need of care.

A charity event was held for children in need of special care

An event for children with special needs was held with the organization of Accelerated Career Academy and the partnership of Founder Women Club.

Meat Donations Were Distributed for Eid al-Adha

On the occasion of Eid al-Adha, the sacrificial meat was distributed in Ismayilli with the joint organization of Founder Club partner businessmen.
