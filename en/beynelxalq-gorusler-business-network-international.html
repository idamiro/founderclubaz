International Meetings - Business Network International

Another international meeting was held between Rich Interior and businessmen visiting Azerbaijan from Dubai under the organization of Founder Club.

Within the framework of the meeting, the guests met with Afsar bey and discussed potential cooperation opportunities.

Projects of mutual interest were shared at the friendly and productive meeting.

Detailed views were exchanged in the direction of future partnership prospects and the development of international business relations.

As the Founder Club, we continue our activities in the direction of establishing international business relations and forming cooperation.
