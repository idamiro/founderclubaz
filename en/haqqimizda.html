Our Mission

Founder Club is the leading business community of Azerbaijan. Our goal is to unite entrepreneurs, stimulate business development and create a healthy competitive environment.

What We Do

The world is changing rapidly

Founder Club has been established since 02.04.2022 in order to support the development of networking activities among companies operating in Azerbaijan, as well as the expansion of relations of partner companies with foreign businessmen.

This network encourages new business opportunities and collaborations by allowing local companies to collaborate with each other. At the same time, the development of relations with foreign businessmen facilitates the access of local companies to global markets.

Founder Club allows its members to expand their business contacts and meet new partners through various events and seminars. This platform supports the development of companies and the increase of competitiveness in the market.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

Creating a strong business ecosystem by uniting Azerbaijani entrepreneurs, facilitating the access of local businesses to international markets and building relationships that bring real value to members.

Our Vision

To be the most influential business community of the South Caucasus, to represent Azerbaijani entrepreneurship at the international level and to create a sustainable development environment for members.

The world is changing. To remain a winner, you need to be able to change with it.
